An essay on the work I do — and the work I used to need done for me.

Acquisitions & Investments

Then.

I was the one staring at a 90-page SPA on a Friday night with no idea where the bear traps were.

Now.

I am the one who redlines it overnight and tells you which five things actually matter.

Raising Venture Capital

Then.

I read my first term sheet and accepted the participating preferred because I didn't know what it meant.

Now.

I know what it means. I know which terms to fight, which to accept, and which to never sign.

Real Estate

Then.

I let a seller pick the title company. I trusted the title company.

Now.

Don't let the seller pick the title company.

Technology Litigation & Disputes

Then.

My code got stolen and I didn't know what a preservation hold was.

Now.

I send the preservation hold by lunch. I image the workstation by sundown.

ADA Digital Litigation Defense

Then.

I would have paid the demand letter.

Now.

I don't pay the demand letter. We fix the site. We file the motion. They walk.

AI & Software Compliance

Then.

I would have read the headline and panicked.

Now.

I read the statute. Most of the panic was the headline.

Data Privacy

Then.

I once shipped a privacy policy in a weekend because the lawyer wouldn't return my emails.

Now.

I return the emails.

Intellectual Property

Then.

I assumed the designer assigned me her logo because I paid for it.

Now.

Without it in writing, she didn't. We fix that, today.

Securities & Quant Trading

Then.

My friends asked me to invest. I would have said yes without thinking about the SEC.

Now.

I think about the SEC first.

Notary Services

Then.

I needed something notarized at midnight in a state with no walk-in notaries.

Now.

I'm a notary. We handle it in the same call.

Technical Criminal Defense

Then.

I assumed an indictment was the end of the conversation.

Now.

It's the beginning of one. I read logs for a living.
